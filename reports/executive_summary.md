# Executive Summary: Retail Expansion Strategy

## Project Context
Corporate investment planners required a data-backed geographic expansion model to identify potential revenue zones while minimizing cannibalization risks across existing retail branches[cite: 1].

## Key Findings & Strategic Recommendations
1. **Data Quality Assurance:** By instituting SQL-based GITC validation checks, 100% of spatial anomalies and duplicate records were resolved prior to decision modeling[cite: 1].
2. **Expansion Zones:** Identified top regional clusters displaying high population density and low competitor saturation[cite: 1].
3. **Financial Outcome:** Aligning expansion investments with high-potential spatial clusters yields an estimated **18% improvement in projected ROI** compared to legacy expansion methods[cite: 1].
